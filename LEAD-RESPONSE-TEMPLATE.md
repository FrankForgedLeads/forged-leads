# Beeyond — Lead Response Playbook

When a new lead comes in through the website, respond **fast** (within the hour if you can).
Speed is the #1 thing that wins the deal. Use the templates below.

---

## Step 1 — Text them first (do this immediately)

A quick text almost always gets a faster reply than email. Send this to their phone number:

> Hey [FIRST NAME], it's Frank from Beeyond 👋 Thanks for reaching out about
> [leads / an estimate]. Just sent you an email too — when's a good time for a
> quick 10-min call today or tomorrow?

---

---

## Golden rules

1. **Speed wins.** First to respond usually gets the deal.
2. **Don't pitch price in the email.** The goal is only to book the call.
   You close on the phone once you understand their needs.
3. **Text AND email.** Two touches beat one every time.
4. **Reply from your own inbox** — never reply to the FormSubmit notification
   address; the lead won't see it.
5. **Book the call, then confirm it.** Once they pick a time, send a one-line
   confirmation so they show up.
