# Beeyond — Square Setup Guide (Website + In-Person Payments)

One account runs everything: website checkout, email/invoice payments, AND
in-person Tap-to-Pay. Same login on the phone app and the computer dashboard.

- **App (phone)** → in-person payments, invoices on the go, sale alerts.
- **Dashboard (computer, squareup.com)** → set up products + website Buy buttons.

---

## Does Square cost me anything?

- **No monthly fee. No setup fee.** You only pay when you get paid.
- In person (Tap-to-Pay): **2.6% + 10¢**
- Online (website button / payment link): **2.9% + 30¢**
- Invoice (emailed): **2.9% + 30¢**, free to send
- Manually keyed card: **3.5% + 15¢**
- **Next-business-day deposit to your bank is FREE** (skip instant deposit, ~1.75%).
- Confirm current rates at squareup.com/pricing.

---

## Step 1 — Sign in on your computer

1. Go to **squareup.com** → click **Sign In** (top right).
2. Use the **same email + password** from your phone app. (Don't make a new account.)
3. You'll land on the **Dashboard**.

## Step 2 — Link your business bank account (if not done yet)

1. Dashboard → **Settings** (or **Balance/Banking**) → **Bank account**.
2. Enter your business **account number + routing number**. This is where payouts land.
3. Square may send two tiny test deposits to confirm — verify them if asked.

## Step 3 — Create your items (packages)

Dashboard → **Items & Orders** → **Items** → **Create an item**. Make these:

**beeyondpro.com**
- `Exclusive Lead` — $125.00 — one-time
- `Growth Plan` — $700.00 — recurring monthly (under Square Subscriptions)

**beeyondestimators.com**
- `Estimate — Under $10k` — $100.00 — one-time
- (Over $10k = 1% → don't make an item; send an Invoice for the exact amount)

## Step 4 — Create the website payment links

Dashboard → **Online** → **Checkout Links** (or **Payment Links**) → **Create**.

1. Pick the item (start with `Exclusive Lead $125`).
2. Choose **Accept a payment** / **Sell an item**.
3. Create → Square gives you a link like `https://square.link/u/xxxxxxx`.
4. **Copy each link and paste it in the chat** so it gets built into the site:
   - Exclusive Lead ($125)
   - Estimate Under $10k ($100)

## Step 5 — Turn on notifications

- The **Square app** already alerts you on every sale.
- Dashboard → **Settings → Notifications** → make sure email-on-payment is ON.

## Step 6 — How to bill the 1% estimator jobs (over $10k)

You don't need a button for these — use an **Invoice**:

1. Phone app or Dashboard → **Invoices** → **Create Invoice**.
2. Enter the client's email, description ("Xactimate estimate — [claim]"),
   and the amount = **1% of the claim value** (e.g. $80k claim → $800).
3. Send. They pay in one tap; money lands in your bank.

---

## What to send back so the sites go live

Paste these links in the chat:
- ✅ Exclusive Lead payment link (`https://square.link/...`)
- ✅ Estimate Under $10k payment link (`https://square.link/...`)

Then the "Buy Now" buttons get built into both websites, and the $700 plan gets
wired to the "Check My Area" flow so you never oversell a territory.

## Safety notes

- Only ever share the **public payment link** — never your password or bank details.
- Square handles all card security. You never store or touch a customer's card number.
